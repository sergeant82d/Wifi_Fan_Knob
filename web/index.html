Fan Profiles

A fan profile holds one fan's measured speeds, plus its own Fan max RPM and presets (below). Auto Configure measures the fan connected now (about 2 minutes: full speed, then slower step by step until it stops) and sets the presets to 25 / 50 / 75 / 100 % of its top speed. You can edit them afterwards; saving keeps them with the active fan.

Without a fan profile, speed is estimated in a straight line between these:

0-255
0-255

Fan Presets

Fan max RPM is your fan's rated top speed: the knob, LCD arc, speed slider and Home Assistant are limited to it. Presets are the speeds for the Low / Med / High / Max buttons here and on the LCD; they must be in order and no higher than the max.

MQTT

The broker Home Assistant uses (e.g. the Mosquitto add-on). The knob announces its controls and sensors to HA by itself (auto-discovery).

Peripheral Power Switch

GPIO 4 transistor that switches the 12 V rail (the fan). The fan controller and sensors stay powered. On while awake, off in standby.
